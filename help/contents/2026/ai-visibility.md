# Win Visibility in the AI Era with Adobe LLM Optimizer & Sites Optimizer

As AI-powered search and discovery reshape how customers engage with digital experiences, organizations must rethink how they optimize both content and websites.

>[!VIDEO](https://video.tv.adobe.com/v/3486903/?learn=on&enablevpops)

This video explore how Adobe Large Language Models (LLM) Optimizer and Sites Optimizer help you improve site performance, and deliver more relevant, high-impact experiences and enhance AI visibility.

In this session, we walk through practical strategies to make your content more discoverable by AI-driven platforms while ensuring your website is fast, structured, and optimized for modern search. Watch to learn how to future-proof your digital strategy and drive better engagement, visibility, and business outcomes with Adobe.

## Key takeaways

* **Understanding the Shift to AI-Driven Search** 
Learn how search is evolving from traditional keyword-based Search Engine Optimization (SEO) to AI-powered discovery, and what this means for your content, visibility, and customer engagement strategies.  
* **Optimizing Content for AI with Adobe LLM Optimizer**
Discover how to structure and enhance your content so it is better understood and surfaced by Large Language Models, improving your brand's presence in AI-generated responses.  
* **Improving Site Performance with Adobe Sites Optimizer**  
Explore how to identify and fix performance, SEO, and structural issues on your website to deliver faster, more seamless user experiences that drive engagement and rankings.  
* **Building an End-to-End Optimization Strategy**
Understand how combining LLM Optimizer and Sites Optimizer enables a holistic approach to digital optimization—connecting content, performance, and AI visibility for better business outcomes.
