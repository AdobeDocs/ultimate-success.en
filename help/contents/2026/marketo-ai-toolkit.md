# The Marketo AI Toolkit: What's Live, What's Next, What to Try First

Marketo Engage already has AI built into the platform itself, not as a bolt-on app or a separate API you have to connect to. Join Zoe and Pramod for a live look at what's actually usable today, across campaigns, content and the everyday admin work that keeps Marketo running smoothly and increases efficiency.

>[!VIDEO](https://video.tv.adobe.com/v/3508118/?learn=on&enablevpops)

Coworker for Marketo Engage (the new name for Marketo AI) puts natural-language answers to your campaign data questions behind the native and callable agents, no report-building required.

The Marketo MCP Server connects the AI tools you already use to Marketo data, and we'll cover the governance and guardrails to have in place before you switch it on.

And the new Email Designer generates on-brand copy, subject lines and images from a single prompt, with Firefly and Adobe Express built into the same flow.

Real workflows, practical use cases and the best practices to get more from what's already live in your instance today. Bring your questions.

This session is for people who run Marketo day-to-day and want less manual work, not another platform to learn.

## Key takeaways

1. Understand the Marketo Native and Callable Agents, AI Coworker natural-language insights.
1. Key considerations around governance and guardrails, and best practises for the Marketo MCP Server.
1. How GenAI helps content creation in Email Designer. Generate on-brand copy, subject lines, and images from a prompt.
