# Connecting AI agentic investments to business impact

Move beyond measuring AI activity and start demonstrating real business impact.

In this session, you will learn a practical framework for connecting AI and agentic investments to measurable outcomes. Explore how to define meaningful KPIs, quantify performance and financial impact, and build a repeatable approach for determining which AI use cases should scale, improve, or stop.

## What You Will Learn

1. How to define meaningful KPIs for AI initiatives.
1. Techniques to quantify performance and financial impact.
1. A structured approach to evaluate and scale successful AI use cases.

## Target Audience

This session is designed for:

- Developers
- Leaders
- Users

## Skill Level

This event is suitable for participants at beginner to intermediate levels.
