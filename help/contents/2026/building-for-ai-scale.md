# From content production to experience production: building for AI scale

Every channel and region shouldn't mean starting from scratch. Join us to explore how organizations can move from project-based content production to reusable, AI-ready experience production, where a single approved source scales into many relevant experiences across channels, regions, and audiences.

## What you will learn

- The key differences between traditional content production and scalable experience production.
- Practical steps to create a reuse-first content foundation.
- Insights into why throughput, not just campaign output, is the clearest signal of AI readiness.

## Target audience

This session is designed for content creators, marketers, and strategists at beginner to intermediate levels who are looking to enhance their content production workflows and prepare for AI-driven scalability.

## Agenda

1. Overview of content vs. experience production.
1. Identifying bottlenecks in your current content supply chain.
1. Building a reusable content foundation: governance, accessibility, and metadata.
1. Practical examples of scalable experience production.
1. Q&A session to address specific challenges and opportunities.

By the end of this session, you'll leave with actionable insights and strategies to modernize your content production processes and align them with AI-driven innovation.
