# Why you want unified review & approvals in Workfront

Watch this webinar on Adobe Workfront Unified Review & Approval to learn how it streamlines content review and approval processes while improving collaboration and efficiency.

>[!VIDEO](https://video.tv.adobe.com/v/3497102/?learn=on&enablevpops)

## Overview

In this session, we provide an overview of the solution, highlight key differences from ProofHQ, and discuss the benefits of adopting Unified Review & Approval. You'll also learn what is required to get started and how to enable the functionality within your organization. Whether you're considering a transition from ProofHQ or simply want to understand the latest capabilities, this session will help you make informed decisions and maximize the value of your Workfront investment.

## What You Will Learn

- How Unified Review & Approval streamlines content processes.
- Key differences between ProofHQ and Unified Review & Approval.
- Benefits of adopting the new workflow.
- Requirements and steps to enable this functionality in your organization.

## Target Audience

- Workfront Administrators
- Developers
- End Users
