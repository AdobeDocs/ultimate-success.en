# AJO code-based experiences: strategies for smarter personalization

Discover best practices for designing, implementing, and optimizing Adobe Journey Optimizer Code-Based Experiences. This webinar covers practical guidance for creating personalized, scalable experiences, avoiding common implementation challenges, and maximizing the value of code-based personalization across digital touchpoints.

>[!VIDEO](https://video.tv.adobe.com/v/3504248/?learn=on&enablevpops)

## What You Will Learn

- **Best Practices**: Learn how to design and implement scalable code-based experiences.
- **Overcoming Challenges**: Understand common issues and strategies to avoid them.
- **Maximizing Impact**: Discover methods to optimize the value of code-based personalization across various digital touchpoints.

## Target Audience

- **Admins**: Insights into managing and deploying code-based personalization effectively.
- **Developers**: Technical guidelines for creating scalable and reusable implementation patterns.
- **Users**: Understanding how code-based experiences enhance customer engagement.

## Agenda

1. **Introduction to Code-Based Experiences**
   - What are code-based experiences?
   - Benefits and use cases.

1. **Designing Scalable Implementations**
   - Creating stable and reusable content structures.
   - Establishing effective governance and ownership.

1. **Implementation Strategies**
   - Client-side, server-side, and hybrid delivery patterns.
   - Integrating with Adobe Journey Optimizer.

1. **Optimizing Personalization**
   - Leveraging customer data for tailored experiences.
   - Testing and validating implementations.

1. **Measurement and Analytics**
   - Tracking user interactions and conversions.
   - Using insights to refine and improve experiences.

Join us to explore how Adobe Journey Optimizer Code-Based Experiences can revolutionize personalization in your digital strategy.
