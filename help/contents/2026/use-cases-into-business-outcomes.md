# Turning use cases into business outcomes

Most organizations accumulate a backlog of use cases for their Adobe environment. The challenge is not imagination but structuring these cases to connect with commercial and operational outcomes that justify investment. This session introduces a practical mapping framework tailored for technical and marketing leadership.

## Mapping capabilities to outcomes that matter

Learn how to transition from "we have AEP and AJO" to "this is what it means for revenue, retention, and cost per acquisition." We provide a repeatable structure that stakeholders can easily follow.

## Prioritizing and sequencing for real-world delivery

Not every use case can take priority. We'll discuss how to evaluate effort versus impact, assess data readiness, and create a realistic roadmap based on team capacity.

## Building the internal case for activation

Discover how to present use case mappings effectively to finance, product, and executive teams, ensuring activation is funded and resourced appropriately.

### Key takeaways:

* Practical tools to align Adobe capabilities with measurable business results.
* Strategies to prioritize and sequence use cases effectively.
* Tips for building a compelling business case to gain stakeholder buy-in.

Join us to unlock a structured approach that bridges the gap between Adobe's technological capabilities and impactful business outcomes.
