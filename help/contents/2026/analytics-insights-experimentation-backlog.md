# Turning analytics insights into an experimentation backlog

Digital experience teams often have access to rich analytics data but lack a clear path from insight to action. This session explores how to systematically mine Adobe Analytics and Customer Journey Analytics for friction points, transform them into prioritized, testable hypotheses, and integrate them into Adobe Target and Journey Optimizer for continuous optimization.

>[!VIDEO](https://video.tv.adobe.com/v/3508245/?learn=on&enablevpops)

## What You Will Learn

In this session, you will:

* Discover how to identify friction points within analytics dashboards.
* Learn to craft testable hypotheses based on data insights.
* Understand how to use Adobe Target to design and deploy tests.
* Explore ways to build and manage a robust experimentation backlog.
* Gain insights into iterating tests to refine and enhance future experiments.

## Target Audience

This session is ideal for:

* Digital marketers looking to enhance their data-driven decision-making.
* Analysts aiming to translate data insights into actionable strategies.
* Teams using Adobe Experience Cloud tools seeking to maximize their impact.

## Agenda

* **Identifying Decision Points**
   * Using analytics dashboards to pinpoint areas of interest.
   * Investigating data to uncover meaningful insights.

* **Creating Testable Hypotheses**
   * Translating data findings into actionable test ideas.
   * Setting up hypotheses for measurable outcomes.

* **Deploying Tests with Adobe Target**
   * Configuring tests for targeted audiences.
   * Executing and monitoring test performance.

* **Building an Experimentation Backlog**
   * Organizing and prioritizing test ideas.
   * Iterating on experiments based on outcomes.

Join us to enhance your skills in turning analytics insights into actionable experimentation, ensuring a continuous loop of improvement and better decision-making.
