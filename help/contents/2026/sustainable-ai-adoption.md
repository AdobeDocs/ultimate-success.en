# AI readiness: preparing for sustainable AI adoption

This session explores the differences between organizations experimenting with AI and those prepared to scale it effectively. It highlights the essential role of ownership, governance, workflow design, data readiness, measurement, and collaboration between business and IT. Learn practical steps to enhance your AI strategy and transform experimentation into measurable business results.

## What you will learn

1. Understand the critical components of AI readiness.
   - Ownership and governance.
   - Workflow design and data readiness.
   - Measurement and collaboration between business and IT.

2. Explore research findings from over 5,000 senior executives on AI adoption.
3. Gain insights into practical strategies for scaling AI initiatives successfully.
4. Learn how to identify and overcome common challenges in AI deployment.
5. Discover best practices for sustainable AI integration into your business processes.

## Target audience

- Developers interested in understanding AI readiness.
- Business leaders looking to scale AI initiatives.
- Users aiming to integrate AI into workflows effectively.

## Agenda

1. Introduction to AI readiness and its importance.
2. Exploring the five stages of AI maturity.
   - From exploring to transforming.
3. Identifying and addressing gaps in AI strategy, people, process, data, and technology.
4. Case studies illustrating successful and challenging AI implementations.
5. Practical steps for planning and executing sustainable AI strategies.
6. Q&A session: Answering your questions on AI readiness and adoption.

This session provides actionable insights and strategies to help organizations effectively prepare for and execute sustainable AI adoption. Join us to transform your approach to AI and drive measurable business impact.
