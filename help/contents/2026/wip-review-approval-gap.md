# From draft to delivered: closing the WIP review and approval gap

Review and approval workflows are often the missing link in the content supply chain, creating bottlenecks, version confusion, and approval challenges. In this session, learn how Adobe Frame.io, Workfront, and Adobe Enterprise Storage work together to streamline collaboration, centralize work-in-progress content, and create a more efficient path from creation to delivery.

## Key topics

- Closing the gap between content creation and asset management  
- Streamlining review and approval workflows with Frame.io and Workfront  
- Managing work-in-progress content as a single source of truth  
- Reducing bottlenecks and improving content velocity
