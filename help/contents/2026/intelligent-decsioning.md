# Powering Real-Time Customer Experiences with Intelligent Decisioning

Delivering personalized customer experiences in real time requires more than just customer data. It relies on intelligent decisioning to evaluate multiple options and deliver the most relevant one at the right moment. Using Adobe Journey Optimizer, brands can structure experiences, apply decision logic, and scale personalization across customer journeys.

>[!VIDEO](https://video.tv.adobe.com/v/3486504/?learn=on&enablevpops)

This session covers the importance of intelligent decisioning, how it works, and its business benefits. Learn how foundational structures like catalog schemas and offer collections streamline scaling, while tools like decision rules and AI models optimize personalized delivery. Discover methods for ranking offers dynamically, including the use of AI-driven models for personalized optimization.

Organizations can achieve impactful outcomes such as increased customer engagement, improved conversion rates, and seamless scalability across channels.
