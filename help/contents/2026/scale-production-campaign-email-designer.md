# Scaling production with campaign email designer & content templates

Discover how Adobe Campaign Email Designer Themes, Content Fragments, and Content Templates help marketing teams scale email production without sacrificing speed, personalization, or brand consistency. This session demonstrates how marketers can streamline campaign creation using drag-and-drop design, responsive layouts, reusable templates, and content fragments.

## Key takeaways

1. **Start from approved templates**: Standardize styling with themes and preview or test email rendering before launch. A scalable process combines marketer flexibility with controlled, reliable output.
1. **Utilize content templates and fragments**: Content templates offer complete email structures, while content fragments provide reusable elements such as headers, footers, promotions, and product blocks. This approach speeds up production while maintaining brand consistency.
1. **Leverage Campaign Email Designer's features**: The drag-and-drop interface supports responsive email creation, personalization, dynamic content, and multiple delivery types, including promotional, transactional, A/B, multilingual, and recurring emails.

## What you will learn

- How to build modern emails using Adobe Campaign Email Designer.
- Creating reusable content fragments and content templates.
- Managing themes for brand consistency across campaigns.
- Testing and validating emails for responsiveness and accuracy.
