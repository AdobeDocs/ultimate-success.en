# Scaling Adobe Agents: Journey Agent

Explore how the Journey Agent combines natural-language workflows with human oversight to accelerate journey creation, analysis, and optimization. Gain practical insights into governance, permissions, recommendation transparency, consent, and review before activation.

>[!VIDEO](https://video.tv.adobe.com/v/3496957/?learn=on&enablevpops)

## What you will learn

- How Journey Agent streamlines journey creation with natural-language prompts.
- The importance of governance and human oversight in AI workflows.
- Practical strategies for optimizing customer journeys.
- Insights into Adobe's AI architecture supporting Journey Agent.

## Target audience

This event is designed for:
- Administrators
- Leaders
- Users

Suitable for individuals at all experience levels, from beginner to experienced.

## Agenda

1. **Introduction to Journey Agent**
   - Overview of AI-driven workflows.
   - The role of human oversight in journey optimization.
2. **Adobe's AI Architecture**
   - Data foundation: AEP and RT-CDP.
   - Coordination layer: Agent Orchestrator.
   - Specialized agents overview.
3. **Deep Dive into Journey Agent**
   - Journey creation, analysis, and optimization.
   - Practical use cases and examples.
4. **Adoption and Readiness**
   - Technical prerequisites.
   - Organizational readiness.
   - Strategic planning for implementation.
5. **Governance and Permissions**
   - Ensuring compliance and brand standards.
   - Transparency in AI decision-making.
6. **Next Steps and Q&A**
   - Phased adoption approach.
   - Immediate actions for organizations.

Join this session to explore how Adobe's Journey Agent can transform your customer experience strategies through advanced AI capabilities.
