# Scaling of Adobe agents | Brand experience agent

See how the Brand Experience Agent can accelerate content production, site modernization, and development while preserving human review and governance. Discover why standardizing high-value, repetitive workflows is essential to delivering scalable business value.

>[!VIDEO](https://video.tv.adobe.com/v/3496956/?learn=on&enablevpops)

## What you will learn

In this session, you will explore:

- How the Brand Experience Agent accelerates content production and site modernization.
- The importance of standardizing high-value, repetitive workflows.
- Ensuring governance while leveraging automation.
- Practical examples and workflows that can be implemented.

## Target audience

This event is designed for:

- Admins looking to optimize workflow efficiency.
- Leaders aiming to scale business processes.
- Users interested in leveraging Adobe Workfront capabilities for better productivity.

## Agenda

1. **Introduction to Brand Experience Agent**
   - Overview of capabilities.
   - Role in content production and site modernization.

2. **Understanding Workflow Standardization**
   - Benefits of repetitive workflows.
   - Real-world examples.

3. **Governance and Human Review**
   - Ensuring compliance and quality control.
   - Integrating human review in automated workflows.

4. **Practical Application**
   - Step-by-step guide to implementing workflows.
   - Q&A session to address specific use cases.

Join us to learn how Adobe agents can transform your workflow processes and enable your team to achieve scalable business value.
