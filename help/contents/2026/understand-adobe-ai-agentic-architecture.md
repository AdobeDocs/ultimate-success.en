# Understanding Adobe's AI and agentic architecture for business needs

This session provides a detailed framework for aligning Adobe's AI capabilities with your business objectives. It focuses on maximizing your Adobe investments and adopting scalable solutions with enhanced data readiness and governance.

>[!VIDEO](https://video.tv.adobe.com/v/3496952/?learn=on&enablevpops)

## What you will learn

- The importance of architecture in leveraging Adobe's AI and agentic capabilities.
- How to align AI implementations with business pillars.
- Strategies for scalable adoption and readiness.
- Insights into Adobe's integrated systems architecture and its application in marketing and creative workflows.
- Key steps for implementing AI agents within your environment.

## Target audience

This session is designed for:

- Administrators
- Developers
- Business leaders
- Users with varying levels of experience (beginner to experienced)

## Agenda

### 1. Introduction to Adobe's AI and agentic architecture

- Overview of architecture's role in business success.
- The connection between AI and business pillars.

### 2. Exploring Adobe's architecture

- Visual representations of Adobe's integrated systems.
- The role of the agent orchestrator in AI workflows.

### 3. Implementing AI agents

- Build paths for AI agents in Adobe environments.
- Steps to ensure effective implementation and governance.

### 4. Best practices and readiness

- Prioritizing use cases and understanding organizational capacity.
- Developing a readiness roadmap for AI and agentic adoption.

### 5. Q&A Session

- Addressing audience questions and providing tailored advice.

Join us for this informative session to enhance your understanding of Adobe's AI capabilities and learn how to apply them effectively to achieve your business goals.
