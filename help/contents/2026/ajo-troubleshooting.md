# AJO troubleshooting: finding the root cause

Learn how to systematically troubleshoot Adobe Journey Optimizer (AJO) by identifying issues, validating assumptions, and addressing root causes. This session covers practical techniques and common challenges to help you improve your AJO troubleshooting skills.

>[!VIDEO](https://video.tv.adobe.com/v/3508248/?learn=on&enablevpops)

## What you will learn

* **A Practical Troubleshooting Approach** Learn how to identify where expected behavior breaks down and validate findings systematically.
* **Audience Qualification & Journey Troubleshooting** Understand what to check when profiles are not qualifying for audiences, entering journeys, or progressing as expected.
* **Decisioning & Push Troubleshooting** Discover how to address unexpected offer decisioning results or push notification behaviors.
* **Eligibility & Compliance** Explore how profile eligibility, consent, and compliance impact qualification and message delivery.

## Target audience

- Developers
- Users at Beginner to Intermediate levels

## Agenda

* **Introduction** Understanding the importance of root cause analysis in AJO.
* **General Troubleshooting Approach** Steps to systematically identify and resolve issues.
* **Audience Qualification** Key validations for audience and journey entry.
* **Decisioning Troubleshooting** Techniques to investigate offer decisioning.
* **Push Notification Troubleshooting** Steps to resolve push notification delivery issues.
* **Compliance Considerations** Addressing eligibility and consent requirements.
* **Q&A Session** Addressing participant queries.

By attending this session, you will gain actionable insights and tools to troubleshoot AJO more effectively, ensuring smoother operations and optimized results.
