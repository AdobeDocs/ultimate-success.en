# AJO product release session

Continue to discover capabilities released over the last 12 months with Adobe Journey Optimizer (AJO). This session highlights new features, including advancements in AI and tools designed to help you deliver value to your customer base or empower your team to work faster. We'll also showcase these features in action with live demos.

## Key discussion points

* What makes AJO stand out
* New features designed to help achieve personalization goals
* Innovations enabling faster value delivery

Join us to explore how these updates can enhance your workflows and help you utilize AJO's capabilities to their fullest potential.
