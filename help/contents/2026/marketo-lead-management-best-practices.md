# Marketo Engage lead management best practices

Discover Adobe's recommended approach to lead management and learn how marketing and sales can work from a shared framework for qualification, follow-up, and measurement.

>[!VIDEO](https://video.tv.adobe.com/v/3502954/?learn=on&enablevpops)

## Overview

This session offers actionable insights to enhance your lead management strategies using Marketo Engage. By aligning marketing and sales processes, you can create a more efficient and effective lead lifecycle. Learn to implement best practices for lifecycle design, lead scoring, routing, and reporting to optimize your marketing and sales efforts.

## What you will learn

- Strengthen lifecycle design, lead scoring, routing, and nurture strategies.
- Enhance consistency across marketing and sales processes.
- Use reporting and key metrics to understand funnel performance and drive better business outcomes.

## Target audience

This session is ideal for:

- Marketing leaders and professionals at all experience levels.
- Sales professionals looking to align with marketing strategies.
- Business leaders aiming to optimize lead management and improve ROI.

## Agenda

1. **Building a trusted lifecycle model**:
   - Understand the six key pillars of a lead lifecycle model.
   - Learn how to create clear stage definitions and align them with marketing and sales processes.

1. **Fit and engagement scoring**:
   - Combine demographic and behavioral data to create a comprehensive lead scoring model.
   - Implement best practices for weighting, negative scoring, and calibration.
   
1. **Lead routing strategies**:
   - Explore account-based matching, territory-based routing, and round-robin distribution.
   - Emphasize accountability and speed in lead follow-ups.

1. **Segmentation and nurturing**:
   - Develop targeted engagement programs based on lead lifecycle stages.
   - Align content with buyer journey stages and use segmentation for personalization.

1. **Lifecycle reporting and optimization**:
   - Differentiate between activity metrics and lifecycle performance metrics.
   - Establish a plan for ongoing model maintenance and refinement.

Join us to enhance your understanding of lead management practices and drive better alignment between your marketing and sales teams.
