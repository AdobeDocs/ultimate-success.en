# AI features in Adobe Experience Manager Assets

Discover how artificial intelligence is revolutionizing digital asset management in Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) Assets. Learn how AI automates tagging, generates metadata, and improves the organization and reuse of assets.

>[!VIDEO](https://video.tv.adobe.com/v/3502952/?learn=on&enablevpops)

## What you will learn

- Understand how Smart Tags support efficient asset categorization.
- Explore how AI-generated metadata enhances searchability and discoverability.
- Learn how improved discovery capabilities can boost content reuse across campaigns.

## Agenda

1. **Introduction to AI in AEM Assets**
   - Overview of AI capabilities in managing the asset lifecycle.

1. **Asset Lifecycle Phases**
   - **Ingestion:** Automating metadata generation, tagging, and color tagging.
   - **Discovery:** Using natural language search and content discovery agents.
   - **Governance:** Ensuring brand compliance and managing digital rights.
   - **Distribution:** Leveraging tools like Content Hub and dynamic media APIs.

1. **Feature Demonstrations**
   - Bulk renaming files.
   - Utilizing Smart Tags and AI-generated metadata.
   - Searching and sharing assets using Content Hub.
   - Applying content optimization agents for channel-ready renditions.

## Target audience

This session is ideal for:

- Marketers seeking to enhance content discovery and reuse.
- DAM administrators aiming to streamline workflows and improve asset governance.
- Content teams looking to reduce manual effort and increase efficiency.

Join us as we explore the innovative AI features in AEM Assets that help organizations manage their digital assets more effectively and efficiently.
