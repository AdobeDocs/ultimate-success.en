# AEM Governance at Scale: Managing Multi-Region Teams and AI-Generated Content

As content operations expand across regions, brands, and now AI-assisted authoring, the key challenge is maintaining control, consistency, and compliance while you scale. Learn how Adobe Experience Manager helps you establish governance that keeps distributed teams aligned and AI-generated content on-brand and on-policy.

>[!VIDEO](https://video.tv.adobe.com/v/3496404/?learn=on&enablevpops)

We'll explore practical frameworks for managing permissions, workflows, and review processes across global teams so you can scale content operations without losing control:

* **Establishing a scalable governance framework** learn how to structure permissions, user groups, and content hierarchies in AEM  
* **Coordinating multi-region and multi-brand teams** discover approaches for managing localization, translation workflows, and approval chains preserving brand consistency  
* **Governing AI-generated content responsibly** understand how to bring AI-assisted authoring into your workflows with the right guardrails review steps, brand and quality checks, and audit trails that keep output accountable
