# Unlocking Adobe's Predictive and Personalisation Capabilities in Customer Experience

Webinar Synopsis: In today's data-driven world, delivering personalised, real-time customer experiences is no longer optional, it's essential. This webinar will explore how Adobe's predictive and personalisation capabilities, embedded across the Adobe Customer Experience Orchestration (CXO) suite, help marketers and business leaders automate decisioning, uncover predictive insights, and scale personalisation with precision. We'll demonstrate how real-time personalisation, automated segmentation, and predictive analytics work within the CXO platform to drive measurable outcomes. We will cover Adobes foundational AI capabilities, ensuring you can confidently position each within your broader strategy. This session will equip you with practical knowledge and real-world examples to drive smarter, faster, and more impactful customer experiences.

## Key Takeaways

* Understand Adobe's predictive and personalisation capabilities across Adobe CXO products  
* Learn how to apply real-time personalisation, predictive analytics, and automated decisioning to drive customer outcomes  
* Explore leading brands use cases and see how they are achieving measurable results  
* Understand Adobe's foundational AI capabilities

## Target Audience

* Product owners and business stakeholders for Adobe CXO platforms  
* Customer experience (CX) and personalisation leaders  
* Business decision-makers, digital transformation and customer strategy leaders  
* Insights and marketing managers
