# Smarter marketing decisions with Adobe Mix Modeler

Learn how Adobe Mix Modeler helps marketers connect marketing investments to business outcomes through a unified approach to measurement and attribution. This session covers key concepts, best practices for data preparation and model validation, and techniques for evaluating channel performance, forecasting outcomes, and optimizing marketing spend.

>[!VIDEO](https://video.tv.adobe.com/v/3502958/?learn=on&enablevpops)

## Key Takeaways

- Best practices for building and validating effective marketing mix models.  
- How to interpret model performance and attribution metrics.  
- Strategies for optimizing budget allocation across channels.  
- An overview of Adobe Mix Modeler workflows, dashboards, and planning capabilities.

## Overview

Adobe Mix Modeler offers a cutting-edge approach to unify marketing measurement techniques, enabling marketers to make data-driven decisions. By integrating marketing mix modeling (MMM) and multi-touch attribution (MTA), this tool provides a holistic view of marketing performance, empowering users to optimize their strategies effectively.

## What You Will Learn

1. **Data Preparation and Model Validation**  
   Understand the importance of high-quality data inputs, including historical data for accurate analysis and stable KPI conversion series.

2. **Model Configuration and Evaluation**  
   Gain insights into configuring models with appropriate granularity, ad stock, and saturation settings for realistic outputs.

3. **Performance Metrics and Model Optimization**  
   Explore how to evaluate models using metrics like R-squared, RMSE, and SMAPE, ensuring reliable predictions and actionable insights.

4. **Business Applications and Attribution**  
   Learn to interpret ROI, incremental performance, and budget reallocation strategies for better marketing outcomes.

## Target Audience

This session is ideal for:

- Marketing leaders and users at all expertise levels (Beginner, Intermediate, Experienced).  
- Professionals seeking to enhance their understanding of marketing performance measurement and optimization.
