# Designing an RTCDP Center of Excellence for Enterprise Scale

How do you turn audience complexity into coordinated action? This practical session gives enterprise architects and platform owners a framework for organizing teams, ownership, governance, activation, and measurement around Adobe Experience Platform and Real-Time CDP so business units can work together with greater clarity and accountability.

>[!VIDEO](https://video.tv.adobe.com/v/3508597/?learn=on&enablevpops)

# Ket Takeaways

* **Clarify audience ownership** Define who creates, manages, approves, and maintains audiences across teams and business units.  
* **Strengthen governance and reduce duplication** Establish shared standards and decision rights to limit overlapping audiences and keep practices consistent.  
* **Make activation accountable** Create clear ownership for audience destinations, activation workflows, and the teams responsible for putting audiences to use.  
* **Coordinate across business units** Set up practical ways for teams to collaborate while balancing enterprise-wide consistency with local needs.  
* **Connect activity to measurement** Align success measures so teams can understand how audiences are activated and whether they are delivering value.
