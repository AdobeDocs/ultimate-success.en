# Strategic planning for CXO success: aligning use cases, architecture, and action

Learn how organizations can align business objectives, customer experience use cases, technology architecture, governance, and action plans into a practical digital strategy roadmap. This event is designed to provide you with actionable insights and strategies to enhance your organization's digital transformation journey.

>[!VIDEO](https://video.tv.adobe.com/v/3502966/?learn=on&enablevpops)

## What you will learn

1. How to align stakeholders around clear business objectives.
1. The process of connecting priority use cases with architecture and governance.
1. Methods to translate strategy into an actionable roadmap focused on measurable outcomes.

## Target Audience

This event is perfect for:

- Business leaders and executives seeking to enhance their strategic planning skills.
- Technology and IT professionals looking to better integrate systems and processes.
- Marketing and operations teams aiming to align workflows with overarching business goals.

## Agenda

1. **Introduction**
   - Overview of strategic planning for customer experience orchestration.
   - Key challenges faced by organizations in aligning technology with business goals.

1. **Core Elements of Strategic Planning**
   - Exploring the relationship between technology capabilities and value realization.
   - Identifying and avoiding common pitfalls such as the tool-first trap, priority sprawl, and fragmented execution.

1. **Use Cases**
   - Defining and prioritizing customer and employee scenarios.
   - Aligning use cases with measurable business outcomes.

1. **Architecture and Roadmap Development**
   - Understanding the role of North Star architecture in strategic planning.
   - Building a roadmap that integrates technology, people, and processes.

1. **Q&A Session**
   - Addressing participant questions and exploring real-world applications of the discussed strategies.

Join us and learn how to effectively connect your digital strategy with measurable outcomes, ensuring your organization remains ahead in the evolving digital landscape.
