# Creating Effective Use Case Statements to Drive Business Value

This interactive workshop walks through a practical framework for turning high-level ideas into clear, measurable use case statements that align to business outcomes. It covers common challenges like vague objectives, lack of prioritization, and difficulty quantifying value, and provides a step-by-step approach to define use cases by identifying the audience, desired behavior change, delivery channels, and KPIs. Real examples and a customer perspective from Palo Alto Networks illustrate how well-defined use cases improve alignment, accelerate execution, and drive measurable impact.
