# Adobe AI Agents in practice: identifying and defining practical use cases

As organizations accelerate their adoption of AI agents, leaders face three critical questions: how to identify the right use cases, how to operate these agents responsibly, and how to measure the value they deliver. This session explores the full lifecycle of deploying AI agents in real business environments, starting with practical methods for spotting workflows where autonomy or intelligent assistance can drive meaningful impact.

>[!VIDEO](https://video.tv.adobe.com/v/3508247/?learn=on&enablevpops)

## What You Will Learn

* **Identifying Use Cases**: Learn how to discover workflows where AI agents can provide the most value, focusing on repeatable and high-volume tasks.
* **Scoring and Prioritization**: Understand methods for scoring and prioritizing use cases based on business value, process clarity, and risk exposure.
* **People and Process**: Explore the roles and governance needed to ensure successful implementation of AI agents.
* **Scaling AI Agents**: Gain insights into creating repeatable processes and scalable solutions through effective governance and monitoring.

## Target Audience

This session is designed for:

* Business leaders looking to integrate AI solutions into their workflows.
* Intermediate users seeking to expand their understanding of AI agent deployment.
* Teams interested in learning how to responsibly implement and scale AI solutions.
