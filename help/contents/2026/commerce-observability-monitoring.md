# Adobe Commerce observability & monitoring best practices

Gain an overview of Adobe Commerce observability and monitoring practices that can improve performance visibility, speed troubleshooting, and support a proactive approach to site health.

>[!VIDEO](https://video.tv.adobe.com/v/3502965/?learn=on&enablevpops)

## Key takeaways

- Understand the role of New Relic and the Site-Wide Analysis Tool.
- Improve visibility into site performance.
- Use monitoring insights to troubleshoot issues faster.

## Overview

This session is designed for Adobe Commerce administrators, developers, leaders, and users at beginner to intermediate levels. It focuses on leveraging Adobe Commerce's monitoring tools to ensure optimal site performance and stability. The session also introduces proactive approaches for maintaining site health and reducing troubleshooting time.

## Topics covered

- **The Role of New Relic:** Understanding how New Relic supports monitoring and troubleshooting Adobe Commerce applications.
- **Site-Wide Analysis Tool:** Learn to utilize this tool for gaining insights into system health and identifying optimization opportunities.
- **Performance Visibility:** Techniques to enhance visibility into site performance metrics and overall health.
- **Monitoring for Troubleshooting:** How to use collected insights for resolving issues promptly and effectively.

## Target audience

This event is suitable for:

- Administrators looking to maintain and optimize Adobe Commerce sites.
- Developers aiming to troubleshoot and improve system performance.
- Business leaders interested in understanding site health and performance metrics.
- Users seeking to learn about Adobe Commerce's proactive monitoring tools.

## Agenda

1. Introduction to Observability and Monitoring in Adobe Commerce.
1. Detailed walkthrough of the Site-Wide Analysis Tool.
1. Insights into New Relic's application and infrastructure monitoring.
1. Practical examples and best practices for troubleshooting.
1. Q&A session to address specific queries from participants.

By the end of this session, participants will be equipped with actionable knowledge to enhance their Adobe Commerce site's performance and health through effective observability and monitoring practices.
