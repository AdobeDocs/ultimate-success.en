# Using GenStudio for Performance Marketing and Marketo for Email campaigns

This webinar explores how Adobe GenStudio for Performance Marketing integrates with Marketo Engage to streamline email campaigns. It demonstrates the setup requirements, including shared IMS org access and the new email designer, and features a live demo showing how GenStudio-created email experiences can be imported into Marketo. The webinar also covers creating email templates in Marketo that are compatible with GenStudio, highlighting the benefits of the integration.

>[!VIDEO](https://video.tv.adobe.com/v/3491514/?learn=on&enablevpops)

A key takeaway is the efficiency and modularity provided by the new email designer, which supports drag-and-drop modules and aligns with Adobe's ecosystem. This integration helps marketers save time, improve collaboration, and deliver targeted email campaigns effectively.
